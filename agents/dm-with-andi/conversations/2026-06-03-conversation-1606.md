**9. The Role of the PRD in the Product vs. Project Model**

The PRD plays fundamentally different roles depending on which model a team operates in. Analyze this distinction and its implications:

**In the Product Model:**
Once product discovery has surfaced an effective solution (*build to learn*), the team moves to delivery (*build to earn*). The primary communication artifact is the **prototype as spec** — the prototype built during discovery communicates what needs to be built. The PRD supplements the prototype by enumerating:
- Specific use cases not easily captured in a prototype
- Non-functional requirements (e.g. scale, performance, security expectations)

The critical constraint: **the PRD must never replace product discovery.** If it does, the team has reverted to the project model.

**In the Project Model:**
The PRD is written *in lieu of* product discovery. The PM assumes they know what is "required" — without validating with customers or through prototyping. This is one of the most common root causes of product failure.
