## The One-Minute Version

Every year, thousands of MBA graduates leave top programs wanting to start a company. Most of them don't.

Not because they lack ambition. Because the friction is invisible and enormous: a legal entity costs €3,000 and three months. An AI agent framework requires a technical co-founder they don't have. A peer network that's actually useful requires curation they can't do alone. A mentor who's done it takes a relationship they don't have.

We're building Entropic to remove that friction. An Entropic Token gives a founder everything they need to start: a shell company, an AI-powered back office, a structured bootstrapping curriculum, and access to a curated community of founders and mentors. AI-native by design, European-first, no equity taken.

We're two founders who've watched this problem from both sides — one from tech and product, one from consulting and deals. We have the first customers already talking to us. We're starting now.

## The Longer Version (Investor / Partner Pitch)

### The Problem

The gap between "I want to start something" and "I have a running company" is wider than it should be in 2026. Not because of capital — early-stage capital is available to the right people. Because of operational overhead.

Setting up a company takes weeks and thousands in legal fees. Building any kind of product requires technical skills most MBA graduates don't have. Finding the right co-founder is basically luck. Getting access to investors or operators who've actually done it is a function of which school you went to and who you happened to sit next to.

This is fixable. AI has dramatically lowered the cost of building operational infrastructure. The tools exist. The question is who bundles them for founders, at the right moment, with the right context.

### The Solution

Entropic is a founder operating system. The core product is the Entropic Token:

**Shell company:** We handle the entity, bank account, and basic legal structure. Off the shelf. Handed to the founder as infrastructure, not as a months-long process.

**AI agent framework:** A pre-configured AI-native back office that runs their company. Invoicing, email, scheduling, research, document drafting — automated from day one. Built on Claude + open tools, extensible.

**Bootstrapping curriculum:** A structured, AI-native learning path from idea to first revenue. Not a course. A founder journey with peer cohort accountability.

**Community + mentoring:** A curated network of founders at the same stage, plus structured introductions to experienced mentors and investors. Quality-controlled — not open access.

### The Market

200,000 MBA graduates per year globally. ~50,000 in Europe. If 20% seriously consider founding, that's 10,000 people per year in Europe alone who are our target. At €2,500 per token, the European opportunity is €250M+ annually.

That's not including the consulting and banking segments, which add another 30,000–50,000 potential customers per year.

### The Business Model

- **Entropic Token (one-time):** €2,000–5,000. Shell company + agent setup + curriculum + 12 months community.
- **Community membership (annual):** €500–1,000. Curriculum + community, no entity setup.
- **Studio Track (equity):** For the strongest founders — we co-invest time and network for 2–5% equity.

Revenue is front-loaded and recurring through memberships. No VC needed to reach profitability.

### Why Us

We are not advisors. We are operators.

One of us has built AI-native products and can build the infrastructure. The other has a direct network into the MBA programs, consulting firms, and investors who are our first customers and first mentors.

We are building something we would have wanted to use. And we have first customers already asking when they can access it.

### What We're Looking For

At this stage: partners, not capital.

We want to connect with:
- MBA program administrators or alumni associations interested in offering Entropic to their graduates
- Consulting firms (McKinsey, BCG, Bain) interested in offering it to alumni going independent
- Experienced operators willing to be part of the expert network
- Founders who've done this and want to give back through mentoring

If there's a capital conversation later — for the Studio Track specifically — we'll have it then.
