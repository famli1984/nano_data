# Vision

*Where we're going. The long game.*

---

## The North Star (5–10 years)

Entropic is the operating system for the next generation of non-technical founders.

In a world where AI removes the technical barriers to starting a company, the remaining barriers are organizational, legal, and social. Entropic removes those. We become the infrastructure layer that ambitious professionals access before they do anything else — the way founders once registered on AngelList or opened a Stripe account.

At scale, Entropic is:
- The standard entry point for MBA graduates going independent
- A portfolio of companies partially backed by the Entropic network (Studio Track)
- A self-sustaining community where senior members mentor junior ones
- A trusted brand in European founder culture — known for quality, not volume

---

## What "AI-Native" Actually Means for Us

AI-native is not a feature. It's the operating assumption:
- The agent framework runs before there's a product
- Founders learn to extend it, not just use it
- Every workflow we teach assumes AI is in the loop
- We build Entropic itself with the same tools we teach

We are not building for the world of 2023. We're building for the world of 2026 and beyond, where not being AI-native is a competitive handicap.

---

## The Slow Burn Bet

We are not optimizing for fast growth or a 3-year exit. We are optimizing for:
- Quality of the community (curation over scale)
- Personal enjoyment of building and running this (it should be fun, not a grind)
- Building something we'd want to be members of
- Reputation that compounds — in 5 years, the best MBA founders know Entropic

The business model supports this: token pricing and community memberships generate real revenue without VC. We don't need to raise to exist.

---

## The Studio Track Upside

Over time, the best founders who come through Entropic become candidates for the Studio Track:
- We co-invest time, network, and operational support
- In exchange: a small equity stake (2–5%)
- No board seat, no control — alignment through skin in the game

If Entropic produces 5 companies per year and 1 in 5 has a meaningful exit over 10 years, the financial upside is real. But this is secondary to the network value.

---

## What We Will Not Do

- Take large equity from founders in exchange for basic access (we're not Y Combinator)
- Optimize for cohort size over cohort quality
- Sell the community or data to third parties
- Build features the community doesn't ask for
- Raise VC ourselves unless the Studio Track demands it

> ❓ Question: When does the Studio Track make sense to start? Probably not Year 1 — we need the reputation first. When is the right moment?
