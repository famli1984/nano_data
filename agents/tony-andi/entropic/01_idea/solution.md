# The Solution Hypothesis

*Last updated: 2026-05-10*

---

## The One-Liner

Entropic gives ambitious first-time founders everything they need to go from idea to running company — legal entity, AI infrastructure, peer network, mentoring — in a single access bundle, without capital and without a technical co-founder.

---

## The Core Product: Entropic Token

An **Entropic Token** is the entry point. It's not a subscription. It's an access unit that bundles:

### 1. Shell Company
- A ready-to-go legal entity (UG or GmbH, DSGVO-compliant)
- Business bank account
- Basic contracts (co-founder agreement template, NDA, ToS)
- Cost: absorbed into the token price, handled in bulk

**Why it matters:** Founders spend weeks and thousands of euros on this. We do it at scale and hand it off as infrastructure.

### 2. AI Agent Framework
- A pre-configured, AI-native operating system for their company
- Automates: invoicing, email responses, scheduling, research, document drafting, competitive monitoring
- Built on open tools (Claude API + agent framework) — no vendor lock-in
- Can be extended by the founder as they learn

**Why it matters:** Most founders drown in admin. This removes the operational overhead that kills momentum in months 1–6.

### 3. Bootstrapping Curriculum
- Structured, AI-native learning path: from idea validation to first revenue
- Covers: customer discovery, pricing, no-code/low-code product building, AI-first go-to-market
- Format: self-paced with peer cohort sync — not a course, a structured journey
- Assessment quiz at entry: places founder on the right track based on stage and skills

**Why it matters:** There's no canonical playbook for bootstrapping AI-native B2B companies. We build it.

### 4. Community Access
- A curated network of founders at the same stage
- Founder matching: peer accountability pairs, co-founder matching
- Expert network: mentors, investors, operators available through structured introductions (not open access)
- Quality over quantity — entry is assessed, not open

**Why it matters:** The right 10 people are worth more than 1,000 random LinkedIn connections.

### 5. Coaching & Mentoring
- Monthly group coaching sessions
- 1:1 mentor matching for specific challenges (fundraising, M&A, product, tech)
- SBI-structured feedback: Situation-Behavior-Impact, not vague advice

## The Assessment Quiz

Entry to Entropic starts with a diagnostic quiz that:
- Evaluates: idea maturity, business model clarity, technical skills, team status, ambition level
- Places the founder in a track: Validate / Build / Scale
- Surfaces immediate next steps — not generic advice but context-specific

> ❓ Question: Should the quiz gate access (you qualify or not) or just route (everyone can enter, but different tracks)?

---

## How It's AI-Native (Not Just AI-Adjacent)

- The shell company + agent framework are designed to run on AI from day one — not retrofitted
- Founders learn to build and extend their own agents — not just use prompts
- The curriculum is built around AI-first workflows: research, writing, selling, operating
- No terminal, no coding required to get started — but depth available for those who want it

## Business Model Hypothesis

| Tier | Price | What you get |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Token (one-time) | €2,000–5,000 | Shell company + agent setup + curriculum access (12 months) + community |
| Community (annual) | €500–1,000 | Community + curriculum only (no entity setup) |
| Expert Network | Referral / rev share | Introductions to investors, mentors, advisors |
| Studio Track | Equity | For the strongest founders: we co-invest time and network for a small stake |

> ❓ Question: Is the token pricing right? MBA students have some money but may balk at €5K upfront. Could a staged model (€500 to start, unlock more) work better?

---

## What We're NOT

- Not an equity-taking accelerator
- Not a course (no certificates, no cohort graduation)
- Not a coworking space
- Not open to everyone — curation is the product

---

## The Insight Behind the Insight

The best founders don't need capital to start. They need momentum. The biggest enemy of momentum is friction: legal overhead, admin, isolation, and not knowing what to do next week.

Entropic removes the friction. The founder provides the ambition.
