# Decision: Start This Venture Together

*Date: 2026-05-10*

---

## Context

Three founders with complementary profiles have identified a gap in the market: ambitious professionals (MBA graduates, consultants, career-changers) who want to start companies but are blocked by the operational mechanics — legal setup, AI skills, peer community, mentoring.

- **Andi** — Idee, Netzwerk, Connecting People, Programmentwicklung
- **Tony** — Rechtsstruktur, Consulting-Netzwerk, Investor Relations (ab 2027)
- **Steffen** — IT-Gründungserfahrung, strategisch-kritisches Korrektiv (Software & AI)

The opportunity is to build a founder operating system that removes those blockers, branded as Entropic. Go-to-market: Seminar "How to Found in the Age of AI" für MBA-Studenten (Uni Pforzheim als Erstpartner).

---

## What We Decided

To start this venture together, with the following shared understanding:

1. **Not primarily about money.** The goal is to build a network, help people we like actually start companies, and enjoy the work. Financial upside is real but secondary.

2. **Bootstrapped first.** We will not raise capital to start. The token model generates revenue from day one. If the Studio Track takes off, we may raise for specific companies within it — but Entropic itself stays capital-light.

3. **Equal partnership, complementary roles.** Nobody is "the boss." Decisions that affect all three require alignment. Day-to-day, each founder operates their domain. Equity: 33/33/33 mit Vesting (Details: `07_founding/equity.md`).

4. **European focus to start.** DACH as beachhead (German-language MBA programs, consulting networks), expand from there.

5. **We build before we sell, but not for long.** Target: first 10 customers within 6 months of this decision. Manual delivery is fine for the first 10.

---

## Options We Considered

- **Join an existing accelerator/community as advisors:** Didn't feel like ours to build.
- **Build a pure SaaS product:** Too tech-heavy, loses the community/mentoring value.
- **Traditional consulting practice:** Not interesting — neither of us wants to bill by the hour.
- **Build it but raise VC:** Premature and unnecessary for the first phase. Changes incentives.
