## Decision: What Type of Entity?

**Options:**

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **UG (haftungsbeschränkt)** | €1 share capital, fast to set up, low cost | "Mini-GmbH" perception, must retain 25% profit until €25K reached |
| **GmbH** | Professional image, standard for B2B | €25,000 share capital required (can be paid in over time), notary cost ~€1,500–3,000 |
| **GbR (informal)** | No setup cost, immediate | Unlimited personal liability, not credible for B2B contracts |

**Recommendation:** Start as **UG**, upgrade to GmbH when revenue justifies it. UG → GmbH conversion is standard.

## Good Leaver / Bad Leaver

Standard German startup practice:

**Good leaver** (illness, death, mutual agreement): Vested shares kept at market value.
**Bad leaver** (resignation within cliff, cause): Unvested shares forfeited, vested shares at nominal value.

Cliff: 12 months. Vesting: 4 years total.
