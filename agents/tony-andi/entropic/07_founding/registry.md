# Commercial Registry (Handelsregister)

For a GmbH or UG, registration is mandatory and takes 2–6 weeks. Not required for a GbR.

*See [entity.md](entity.md) for the decision on which legal form to use.*

---

## Steps

1. Draft articles of association (Gesellschaftsvertrag) — notary required
2. Open a business bank account and deposit share capital
3. Notary submits registration to local Amtsgericht
4. Await confirmation — receive Handelsregisternummer
5. Notify Finanzamt — receive Steuernummer
6. Apply for USt-IdNr. (VAT ID) — needed for B2B invoicing in the EU

## Notes

- UG: minimum €1 share capital. GmbH: minimum €25,000 (can be paid in installments as Sacheinlage or cash over time).
- Notary cost estimate: €800–2,500 depending on complexity and share capital.
- Timeline: 2–6 weeks from notary submission to Handelsregisternummer.
- Registered address can be a home address or a virtual office address (e.g., Regus, ebuero).
