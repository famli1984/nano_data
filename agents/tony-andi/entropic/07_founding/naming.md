# Company Name & Domain

*Brainstorming started: 2026-05-10*

---

## What Makes a Good Name for Entropic

- Works in English (international from day one — MBA programs, consulting networks)
- Signals transformation, not just tools — we don't just teach, we change what's possible
- Short enough for a GmbH/UG suffix without sounding corporate
- Domain available as `.io` or `.com` (international audience, not `.de`-first)
- Not already a registered trademark

> ⚠️ Domain availability ≠ company name availability. Check [handelsregister.de](https://www.handelsregister.de) and [dpma.de](https://www.dpma.de) before registering.
