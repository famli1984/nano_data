# Market Size

*Last updated: 2026-05-10 — First estimates, to be refined with data*

---

## Framing the Market

Entropic doesn't fit neatly into a single market category. We span:
- Founder education & community
- Legal entity setup
- AI productivity tooling for startups

The addressable market depends on how broadly we define "aspiring founder with business acumen."

---

## TAM — Total Addressable Market

**Definition:** All professionals globally who could plausibly want what Entropic provides.

Inputs:
- ~250,000 MBA graduates per year globally (top 100 programs)
- ~500,000 management consultants globally (McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Big 4 + boutiques)
- ~200,000 investment bankers / PE / VC professionals
- ~1M "intrapreneurs" and senior corporate professionals seriously considering founding

**Rough TAM:** 1.5–2M people per year who are the profile.
At €2,000 average token price → **€3–4B annual TAM** (very rough, global)

This is a framework number, not a planning number.

---

## SAM — Serviceable Addressable Market

**Definition:** The subset Entropic can realistically reach — European focus initially, English-language, digital-first.

Inputs:
- European MBA graduates: ~50,000/year (INSEAD, LBS, IE, WHU, HEC, Bocconi + others)
- European consultants considering independence: ~30,000/year (estimate)
- DACH region specifically: ~10,000/year as primary focus

At €2,000–3,000 per token, assuming 5–10% conversion of engaged audience:
**SAM: €100M–500M/year** in Europe at maturity — very rough

---

## SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market (Year 1–2)

**Realistic near-term target:**

- Goal: 100 Entropic Tokens sold in Year 1
- At €2,500 average: **€250K revenue**
- Community memberships (€500/year): 300 members → **€150K**
- **Year 1 target: €400K total revenue**

This is achievable without VC, without marketing spend, purely through Tony's network + first-doer community + word of mouth.

Year 2 target: 300 tokens + 1,000 community members → €1–1.5M revenue

## Beachhead Sizing

**INSEAD alone:** ~1,100 MBAs/year. If 20% are seriously considering founding: 220 people per cohort. If Entropic captures 10% of those: 22 customers per cohort from one school.

Tony's direct network: estimate 200–500 senior professionals who would take a conversation seriously. If 10% convert at some level: 20–50 in Year 1.

**First 10 customers:** Should come entirely from direct relationships. No marketing needed for the first 10.

## Key Assumptions to Validate

1. Willingness to pay €2,000–5,000 for the token (not a subscription — a one-time bundle)
2. MBA students and post-consultants are underserved (not served by existing programs)
3. European focus is right — or should we start US-first with Tony's network?
4. Token pricing model vs. recurring subscription — which converts better?
