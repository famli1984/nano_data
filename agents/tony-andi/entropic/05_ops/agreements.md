## Decision-Making

- **Day-to-day (within your domain):** Each founder decides. No approval needed.
- **Cross-domain or major decisions** (pricing, hiring, pivots, equity, new products): Alignment required from both.
- **Deadlock:** If we disagree, we write up both positions in `04_decisions/` and sleep on it for one week. If still stuck, we bring in a trusted third party as a sounding board.

**What counts as a major decision:**
- Changing token price by >20%
- Adding a new product line
- Any equity change
- Any expense >€5,000
- Any external commitment made in Entropic's name

## Communication Norms

- **Async by default.** This repo is the source of truth — not WhatsApp threads or email chains.
- **Decisions in writing.** If it's not in `04_decisions/`, it didn't happen.
- **Weekly check-in:** 30 minutes, standing agenda (see `rituals.md`)
- **Response time expectation:** 48 hours for anything in this repo. Real-time not expected.
- **Conflict:** If something bothers you, raise it within one week. Don't let it fester.
