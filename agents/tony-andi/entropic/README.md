# Entropic

A co-founder workspace for building a startup studio that helps ambitious professionals — MBA students, career-changers, aspiring founders — actually start something.

Not an incubator. A **founder operating system**.

---

## What We're Building

Entropic is an AI-native platform that bundles everything a first-time founder needs to go from idea to running company — without capital, without connections, and without knowing how to code.

The core product is the **Entropic Token**: a bundled access unit that gives founders:
- A shell company (legal entity, bank account, basic structure — ready to go)
- An AI agent framework that automates the operational work of running a company
- A structured learning path: how to bootstrap, validate, sell, and scale without VC
- Access to a curated peer community — MBA-grade ambition, no corporate bullshit
- Mentoring, coaching, and network introductions through the Entropic community

Our beachhead: MBA students and recent graduates who are serious about founding but don't know where to start.

## How We Work

- Everything important goes in writing, in this repo
- Decisions get their own file in `04_decisions/` — context, options, outcome
- Open questions: `> ❓ Question: ...` anywhere in any file
- Weekly check-in: async update + 30-min sync
- Neither founder acts alone on major decisions (entity, equity, hiring, pivots)
